WHAT IT DOES

Useful geometry, humble acoustics.

Drop QUIET targets where sleep, work, calls, or calm matter. Drop NOISE sources where a TV, speaker, appliance, fan, or other noisy object might sit.

HUSH//MAP paints a relative impact field using distance and simple barrier penalties. Tap a noise source to select it, then ask the app to search the room for a lower-impact location.

Walls here are planning barriers, not material-specific acoustic simulations. Doors, reflections, low-frequency vibration, floors, ceilings, and construction details can dominate real sound.

The goal is not “this will be 42 dB.” The goal is “this corner is probably kinder to the places I care about.”
