CONTINENT INTERVENTION

Build 047 keeps the 046 continent but makes the world order rotatable. The initial row-major arrangement is W1 W2 W3 / W4 W5 W6 / W7 W8 W9. Each ROTATE WORLDS press performs the cyclic shift 123456789 → 912345678 and replays from tick 0. The same original W5 founder cohort is always placed in the center chunk; the world habitats rotate around that fixed population. The other eight chunks begin biologically virgin.

Each chunk is still 32×24×8 and is generated from its original deterministic world seed. Rotation changes placement only; it does not rotate or mutate a habitat internally. The W5 founder genomes and post-founder simulation RNG state are reproduced independently of habitat placement, so the same starting population can be exposed to whichever world occupies the center slot. The intervention is the horizon: internal X/Y chunk walls are traversable; only the outside edge of the 96×72×8 continent is closed.

The old soft population gate becomes 180 per destination chunk. It gates births, not migration, and same-tick births can still overshoot because scheduled newborns are appended after the parent pass. A parent at a border may place offspring directly into a neighboring chunk if that destination is below its birth gate.

The field guide and fossil observer remain passive. New migration measurements record first entry, first local birth, chunk crossings and chunks visited. No observer calls RNG or changes ecological state.
Observer-only: camera rotation, field visibility, motion persistence, slice focus, lineage filtering, centroid calculations and seed selection never feed back into the volume. The organisms do not know they are being rendered.
