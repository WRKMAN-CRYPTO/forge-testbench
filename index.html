WRKMAN CRAFT

Daylight is your work window. Night gets colder. Your builds help, but they do not work forever.

  • Gather wood and scrap with E.
  • Fires burn fuel. Stand near one and press E to feed it 1 wood.
  • Lean-tos restore some Grit during daylight.
  • Each night bites harder. Survive 3 nights.
