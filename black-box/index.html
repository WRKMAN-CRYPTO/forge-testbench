THE RULE

    
Evidence before stories.

    
BLACK BOX inspects public Solana transaction data. It can prove things the chain records: which accounts signed, token/SOL balance changes, parsed transfers, approvals, revocations, authority changes, fees, and inner instructions.

    
It cannot prove who physically held a private key, whether a signer was tricked, or whether a website was malicious from chain data alone. When two explanations remain possible, it says so.

    
Never enter a seed phrase or private key. This tool has no reason to ask for one.
