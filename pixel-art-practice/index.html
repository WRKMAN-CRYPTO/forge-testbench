EXPERIMENT 001

Silhouette Hunt

Silhouette separation
PLAY →
LESSON STUDIED

Block the silhouette first. At 16×16, exaggerate the pose and class-defining prop; use asymmetry and empty space so identity survives before color and internal detail.

WHAT I PRACTICED

Four original classes built around external shape: a shield-and-sword knight, tall-hat mage with staff, archer whose bow creates open negative space, and a broad brute with an oversized club.

SELF-CRITIQUE

The archer is the strongest read because the bow creates unmistakable negative space. The brute gets useful width, but its club is too visually close to the body. The mage hat helps, though the staff is still generic. The knight has recognizable equipment but its sword and shield could be pushed farther away from the torso. Biggest weakness: all four bodies share too much of the same central humanoid mass. Next silhouette study should alter stance and torso geometry more aggressively.
