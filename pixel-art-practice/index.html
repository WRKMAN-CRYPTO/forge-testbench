One deliberate lesson per run. The central test is recognizable 16×16 form, especially in silhouette. Ratings stay only on this device.

EXPERIMENT 007

Hidden Limb

Anatomical compression through occlusion
PLAY →
PRINCIPLE STUDIED

At tiny scale, not every limb should earn a separate outer contour. Let rear limbs overlap the torso and reserve silhouette space for the limb that actually defines the action.

WHAT I ATTEMPTED

Four original 16×16 worker poses: run, duck, brace, and jump. The rear arm is deliberately swallowed by the torso in several poses; the brace pose uses one combined forearm block instead of two arm branches. An INK TEST removes color instantly.

SOURCE

GameDev Academy: Create A Pixel Art Player Sprite — its small-sprite construction explicitly overlaps the rear arm with the body rather than outlining every limb.

SELF-CRITIQUE

Run and brace are the cleanest human reads and avoid the antenna problem. Duck compresses too aggressively and can become a low creature/blob in silhouette. Jump is cleaner than Run 006 but the leg relationship is still weaker than the torso/head read. This is progress in subtraction, not rival-class work yet.

BENCHMARK FLAGS

No obvious extra-head branch in the specimen strip. Main remaining risk: limb-count ambiguity in jump and loss of human identity in duck. Rival pressure remains below competitive.
