Training pressure: CORE ONLY removes tails, ears, horns, and decorative tufts. Recognition must come from the big masses: torso depth and length, shoulder height, neck length, and head-to-body ratio. FULL FORM restores small hooks only as a control. If an animal becomes recognizable only after FULL FORM, the core construction failed.
