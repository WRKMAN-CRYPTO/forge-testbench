WINDUP LANE

Read the boss silhouette before the strike. Move out of the threatened lane before impact.

ATTACK 1/10HP 3SCORE 0
Training pressure: the boss has three attack lanes. CLEAR WINDUP gives each startup a distinct whole-body silhouette that points toward the future hit. MUDDY WINDUP compresses all three startups toward the same generic crouch. The strike itself is intentionally fast. If you only understand the attack after impact, the anticipation failed.
