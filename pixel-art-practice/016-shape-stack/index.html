SHAPE STACK

Four props, no anatomy. Each 16×16 silhouette is organized as one dominant mass, one supporting mass, and one small accent. The test is whether unequal size hierarchy makes the object readable before color or surface detail.

Drill: identify the prop from broad form. LANTERN spends most pixels on the chamber, then roof/base, then a tiny crown. ANVIL spends them on the long top slab, then waist/base, then horn taper. DRILL uses motor body, handle, then bit. CANNON uses barrel, wheel carriage, then muzzle/tail accents. INK removes internal color. SMALL reduces the sprite toward actual game-size pressure. If the small accent becomes the identity, the hierarchy failed.
