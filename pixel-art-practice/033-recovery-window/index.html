033 // RECOVERY WINDOW

Four tiny fighters attack. Tap COUNTER only while the silhouette is visibly recovering, before it settles back to ready.

WATCH THE BODY
Impact is dangerous. Recovery is your opening.

Principle: recovery is a gameplay state, so its key pose needs a distinct silhouette. Keep momentum visible after impact, but do not let recovery collapse into either the strike pose or neutral idle.

No color cue tells you when to tap. The body and weapon relationship must carry the state.
