ARMORY GRAMMAR

Weapon identity should come from mass distribution and proportion before material or ornament. Point, head, shaft, guard, and handle each get a distinct silhouette job.

Drill: all four are original 16×16 weapons. The silhouette must identify the family before shading. Sword uses a broad taper plus cross-guard; axe puts most mass at one side of the shaft; spear spends almost everything on a long axis plus one sharp head; mace uses a compact heavy head and narrow handle. Hard fail: two weapons collapse into the same black shape.
