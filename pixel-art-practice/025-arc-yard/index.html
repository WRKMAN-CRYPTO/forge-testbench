Training pressure: every machine has fixed endpoints. ARC LOCK redraws the in-between silhouettes around a stable pivot, keeping link length and attachment believable. CHORD DRIFT uses naive straight-line in-betweens that shorten, slide, or detach the moving mass. The test is whether motion preserves the machine's construction, not merely whether frame one and frame four look good.
