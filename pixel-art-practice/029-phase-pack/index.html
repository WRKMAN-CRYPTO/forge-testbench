PHASE PACK

Can mechanical phase discipline make tiny quadrupeds move without turning their legs into an unreadable picket fence?

WHAT CREATURE READS?
WOLF?
ROUND 1/12
SCORE 0
Training pressure: each animal has four 16×16 gait frames. PHASED offsets fore and hind leg actions instead of moving every leg together. SYNC deliberately makes the limbs hit the same phase, which should feel pogo-like and create denser, less informative silhouettes. INK removes color. The creature must keep its identity through every frame, not only in the prettiest contact pose.
