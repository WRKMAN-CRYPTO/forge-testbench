Magenta is truly instruction-free: an agent keeps moving under its own momentum and may enter another grayscale island. There are no invisible canvas walls: agents can fly offscreen, remain alive, and continue drifting beyond the map. Tap RESEED to bring them back. No pink is drawn into the spell. Architecture: This field is a control surface, not a sprite. The visible spell is composed of moving hex agents. The editor and simulation share a portable 1,024-value map. Separate experimental lab, no roguelike integration.
