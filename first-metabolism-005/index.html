Catalyst reproduction: catalyst + block + food → 2 catalysts + waste. Repair: catalyst + block + food → catalyst + membrane + waste. Decay converts catalysts and membrane into waste. No free repairs or spontaneous catalysts.

Coarse-grained reaction bookkeeping, not atomistic chemistry. Food is modeled as one conserved material unit that becomes waste when spent. A catalyst or membrane unit contains one building-block unit. The boundary's permeability is a model assumption.
