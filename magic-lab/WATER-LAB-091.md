# WANDRUNE 091: WATER LAB

**Status:** Implemented only on the isolated `forge-testbench` VFX branch. Do not merge these changes into the Wandrune production game without iPhone evaluation.

## Starting point

The existing Wandrune element-pixel system already recognizes **water** as element ID 2 and gives it canonical `wandrune-mat069-water` appearance. The crafted spell drawing pass submits water agents to the renderer with exact world-space positions, actual direction `angle`, and sampled trail coordinates. No new gameplay element was added.

## New rendering-only options

1. **WATER DROPS: ON/OFF:** Three cached 64x64 pearl-blue droplet silhouettes replace the older faceted water surface. Their narrow point is authored at the top; visual rotation follows `record.angle + PI/2` and a very small wavering. Rounded rears, transparent aqua shading, glassy rims, and clipped glints distinguish water from fire and ice. A small amount of the original water material is embedded once per cached variant. Existing Sprite/ParticleContainer batching, alpha fade, render count, world coordinates and all spell logic are untouched. OFF restores the canonical source texture.
2. **WATER STREAMS: ON/OFF:** One reusable Pixi Graphics layer draws two thin translucent refractive ribbons along each selected water agent's **real recent world-space trail**, with up to 64 trails per frame. No guessed connections between agents, no additional physics agents, no fluid simulation, no new field sampling, and no additional graphics layers per path. Invalid, too-long and faded paths are ignored. OFF clears the layer immediately.
3. Both options work with water generated by the existing element-pixel map. Query values `?waterDrops=0` and `?waterStreams=0` allow clean A/B comparison. Fire art and the 088c accepted individual flame direction remain unchanged. Frost and lightning are untouched.

## Test results

**16/16 headless suites passed: 1,979 assertions.** New `pixi-water-vfx.tests.js` passes 37 checks, including independent toggles, 200 mixed water/frost/fire agent records, unchanged source data, original material fallback, no per-frame texture or particle allocation, water-only real path strokes, stream budget, zero-alpha fading, and cleanup. Existing fifteen suites cover material fidelity, fire effects, stability, cameras, bridge, Pixi batching and 088c rotation.

Headless tests cannot verify the actual water appearance or iPhone GPU frame rate.

## Phone test

- In the Magic Lab element pixel painter, choose **WATER** (element 2), make a water-agent recipe, and cast it in the isolated preview.
- Watch a few isolated water agents, a long spray of water, and 2–3 concurrent casts.
- Toggle **WATER DROPS** alone: do the new particles read as water rather than blue flames, arrows, or frost shards?
- Toggle **WATER STREAMS** alone: do paths look like a silky liquid wake, or too much spaghetti?
- Confirm alpha fading and nearby frost/fire spells remain unchanged.

This is the first water *visual* trial, not a new fluid-mechanics simulation.
