The automatic ramp measures frame cadence and full JavaScript update + render call, and stops after two successive stages below the target (50 FPS or P95 over 25 ms). What it cannot test: actual GPU execution time, device temperature, thermal throttling over long sessions, or Wandrune gameplay. A benchmark ceiling is not necessarily a crash point. Defined adjusts blending, opacity and glow without reducing sprite counts. World zoom changes visible fraction, so compare FPS only at matching camera and scene settings.
