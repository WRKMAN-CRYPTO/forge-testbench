Research notes

The same 16-frame right-shift and opacity compositor is used to create the two sensory signals. Original brightness is either equal or independently randomized. The learner sees only the resulting RGB pair and gets correctness feedback during training. Evaluation does not update it.

A single image cannot always distinguish an old bright point from a newer dim point. Therefore 100% accuracy may be impossible in the randomized condition. Importing weights from Eye 003 tests transfer; it does not transfer previous experiences or memory frames.
