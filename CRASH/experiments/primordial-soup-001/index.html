Agents inherit and mutate movement, foraging, and sharing tendencies. This is selection, not within-lifetime learning. Resource supply is capped; energy cannot be created by sharing.

Experimental boundary

Agents pay energy to exist and move, collect nearby food, may share with nearby neighbors, reproduce when sufficiently energetic, and disappear at zero energy. The laboratory defines these rules. Agents have no language, explicit survival objective, or neural learning.
