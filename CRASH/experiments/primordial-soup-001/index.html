Experimental boundary

Agents pay energy to exist and move, collect nearby food, may share with nearby neighbors, reproduce when sufficiently energetic, and disappear at zero energy. The laboratory defines these rules. Agents have no language or explicit survival objective. Work presents raw RGB channel values for both squares, with positions randomized each round. Agents learn color-channel weights from energy feedback, without receiving color names or the correct side. This is RGB-feature learning, not pixel-based visual perception.
