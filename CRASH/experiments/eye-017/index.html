Audit report

Evaluate to inspect accuracy and contradictions.

The identical-twin condition deliberately assigns opposite labels to identical pixels. Its maximum expected accuracy is 50% on balanced pairs, regardless of model power. The visible-clue condition is solvable because a cyan pixel is placed immediately toward the chosen candidate. Both learners use the same simple logistic classifier over engineered marker-neighbor features. The cyan feature is supplied as a numeric input: this is a task-identifiability test, not discovery of color or shape.
