Observatory

Zero initial velocity, temperature, and light. Cold agents are invisible in normal view; inspection mode reveals them without changing the physics. Core/outer metrics compare the nearest and farthest 25 agents from the population centroid. All agents attract all others. Solid-sphere collisions ricochet with 82% restitution; lost kinetic energy becomes heat. Contact conducts heat. Hot agents expand by at most 1%. Radiation travels through empty space, transfers energy only when intercepted by an agent, and escapes at the walls.

Dimensionless experimental units. Energy ledger includes signed attractive potential; numerical drift is displayed, not hidden. Expansion work and photon momentum are approximated, so this is a research toy rather than a strict thermodynamics solver.
