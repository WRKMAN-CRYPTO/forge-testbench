Experiment notes

Stationary bright pixels contribute repeatedly and become clear. Moving pixels distribute their contribution along a trail. Brief flashes tend to stay dim, although repeated flashes can still accumulate. A blinking pixel and a consistently dimmer pixel can produce the same total.

The contrast-expanded display is for the observer only. It does not modify Crash's stored data. The archive stores ten-frame sums, not the original ten snapshots. No labels, target positions, or handcrafted change detectors are provided to a learner because there is no learner in this experiment.
