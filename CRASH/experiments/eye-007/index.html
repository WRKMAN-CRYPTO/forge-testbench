Equipment versus learning

Both learners see eight complete 32×32 RGB frames. A fixed change detector sums new pixel intensity separately within the left and right halves. The old learner uses seven signed change magnitudes with its imported Eye 006 weights, unchanged during Eye 007 tests.

The new learner receives a seven-step binary change-presence history, so dim and bright appearances count equally. It learns how to weigh that history through reward, without receiving event times or labels as features. The detector, binary normalization, spatial halves, and ordered memory are built-in equipment, not discovered by Crash.

The two events always occupy opposite halves and persist. Success here tests robustness to variable onset timing, not independent discovery of objects, spatial regions, or memory. The old learner must be imported to make a genuine Eye 006 transfer comparison.
