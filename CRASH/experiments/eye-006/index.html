What is learned?

Crash receives eight full RGB frames with two points appearing at random coordinates in the left and right halves. No point coordinates are passed to either learner. A fixed, general-purpose change detector compares successive pixels and sums positive changes within each half. The learner must discover how to weigh those changes across the eight frames to predict which half contained the first appearance.

The change detector, left/right grouping, and ordered frame buffer are supplied equipment, not learned abilities. The last-frame control sees only final pixel brightness. A high score shows learning to use detected changes, not autonomous object discovery.
