How this little mind works


Word agents track terms Red encounters. Experience agents keep whole observations and their order. Association agents emerge from repeated transitions. They aren't individually powered by an LLM.


The engine applies weighted word overlap to find earlier contexts, then considers what followed those experiences. Predictions include supporting evidence and their competing outcomes. The confidence score is a heuristic, not a calibrated probability.


Surprise occurs when a new event contradicts a reinforced prediction. Rather than deleting the older prediction, the engine records the new outcome, allowing competing associations to develop.


Recall reads remembered experiences and their neighbors without inventing facts. New episode stops temporal connections from crossing unrelated sessions. All tests use exactly the same general engine, independently from the live brain.


Limitations: This is token similarity, not true semantic understanding. It's not an autonomous computer user, cannot watch Red's desktop yet, and processes no new events while closed.


Bridge interface: RedMemoryV2.observe({type:"ACT",text:"press key"}), .startEpisode(), .recall("press key"), .predict("press key"), .export(), .import(snapshot).
