BEST APPROACH

Rectangular rigid objects only. Door thickness, knobs, trim, hallways, flexible cushions, and 3D corkscrew moves are outside this model.

WHAT IT KNOWS

A useful yes, not a magical no.

DOOR//FIT tests each rectangular face of the object against the clear doorway opening while rotating that face from 0° to 90°.

If it finds a fit, that gives you a simple orientation that clears on paper. If it finds none, the object may still pass with a true 3D tilt, removable feet, compressed cushions, or a different route.

Measure the narrowest clear opening, not the door slab. Use the clearance reserve to keep a little breathing room for imperfect measurements and human hands.
