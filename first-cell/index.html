Pulse the membrane, then watch its voltage and channel states. Train ×100 alters its history. Probe A/B compares trained and untouched states under identical inputs.

Model: two ion reservoirs, voltage-dependent channels with slow adaptation, a fuel-limited ion pump, and passive leakage. Conservation is enforced for modeled ions. ATP and energy are illustrative proxies, not physical units. No behavior or learning objective is scripted.
Tap the cell to pulse it. Time is simulation time, not real biological time.
