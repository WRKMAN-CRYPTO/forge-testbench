PHYSICS, NOT OBJECTIVES

Seeds lose energy simply by existing and acting. Reaching the split threshold copies a seed; energy is conserved across the split. Nothing awards points for moving, touching, making words, changing cells, or resembling a program.

The four field types are merely different energy-transfer mechanisms: α stores replenishing charge; β oscillates and transfers energy only under a matching internal bit; γ stores a writable bit and finite change-energy; δ couples energy transfer to motion direction. These are environmental affordances, not named jobs.

Build 037 changes only the ecology: leak is slightly lower, renewable field recovery is slightly higher, and maximum lifespan is longer. Eight deterministic world seeds provide different landscapes and founder genomes under identical laws. Offspring still inherit executable genomes with mutation, condensation and occasional recombination.
Observer-only milestones: conserved words, conserved external-action words, and the first lineage that becomes established are recorded but never feed back into survival or reproduction. Different worlds change starting conditions, not the laws.
