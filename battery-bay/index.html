Scope: disposable 1.5 V alkaline AAA / AA / C / D only.

METHOD

Loaded test wins.

Open-circuit voltage can bounce back after use, so it is only a rough screen. A brief loaded reading tells you more about whether the cell can still do work.

  1. Use only intact 1.5 V alkaline AAA, AA, C, or D cells.
  2. Set the meter to DC volts.
  3. For a DIY test, place about 10Ω across the cell for 1–2 seconds and read voltage while loaded. Use a resistor rated 0.5 W or higher.
  4. Enter the loaded reading and let BATTERY//BAY route it.
Loaded routing

≥ 1.35 V → READY
1.10–1.34 V → LOW-DRAIN
< 1.10 V → RECYCLE

The 1.35 V cutoff is a practical sorting heuristic, not a manufacturer state-of-charge specification. Never test leaking, swollen, hot, corroded, or damaged cells. Do not use this tool for rechargeable, lithium, coin, button, or 9 V batteries.
