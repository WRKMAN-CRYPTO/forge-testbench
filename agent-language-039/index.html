ASSAY DESIGN

NATIVE keeps the genome unchanged.
EXPANDED replaces every target word with its primitive sequence, preserving primitive order but losing one-tick compression.
SCRAMBLED keeps one macro token and the same primitive counts/cost, but changes their internal order.
NULL SLOT keeps the one-tick genome slot but executes no primitive operations.
DELETED removes every copy of the target word from the hereditary cycle.

All clones begin at the specimen's same cell, direction and internal bit in independent copies of the same world snapshot. They are sterile: no births, mutation, recombination or competition.

INTERPRETATION GUARDRAIL

A difference here establishes a causal effect of this executable hereditary unit in this starting context. It does not prove the word was selected because of that effect, that the effect generalizes to other cells, or that the word has a human-like meaning. EXPANDED tests the value of compression/timing. SCRAMBLED tests ordering while holding macro timing and primitive counts fixed. NULL SLOT and DELETED test removal under two different cycle-length controls.
This build is a laboratory copy. Watching or breaking genes here cannot feed back into Seedbed.
