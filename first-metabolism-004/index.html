A finite starting population of catalysts consumes food to create new catalysts and membrane material. Every catalyst has a limited lifetime. Membrane decays and leaks. No automatic reseeding, no goal-directed control, no reproduction routine.

This is an intentionally abstract stochastic chemistry experiment, not atom-scale molecular dynamics. Persistence is not equivalent to life.
